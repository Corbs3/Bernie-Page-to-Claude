# Bernie: privacy policy

**Effective date:** 17 November 2026

This policy explains what the Bernie Chrome extension ("Bernie") does with information when you use it.

## Who publishes Bernie

Bernie is published privately for staff of our organisation through the Chrome Web Store.
For questions about this policy, contact: **[YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]**

## The short version

- Bernie only reads a page when **you** ask it to.
- Bernie only passes page text to **Claude**, and only when you ask.
- Nothing is sent to Claude until **you** press Enter.
- Bernie has **no servers, no analytics and no tracking**. The developer never receives your data.

## What information Bernie handles

When you click Bernie, use the right-click menu or press the shortcut, Bernie reads:

- the **text of the current page**, or just the **text you have selected**
- the **page title** and **web address (URL)**

Bernie does not read pages in the background, and does not collect your browsing history, passwords, form entries, cookies or personal details.

## How that information is used

Bernie uses it for one purpose only: to give the page to Claude so you can ask about it. Depending on the option you choose:

- **Browser:** Bernie opens a new chat on claude.ai and pastes the text into the message box.
- **Mac app:** Bernie hands the text to the Claude desktop app on your computer using a Claude link. Long pages are copied to your clipboard instead.
- **Paste myself:** Bernie copies the text to your clipboard so you can paste it wherever you choose.

In every case, the text sits in the message box until **you** decide to send it.

## Sharing with others

- Bernie does **not** sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
- When **you** send a message in Claude, it is handled by Anthropic under its own terms and privacy policy, and any agreement your organisation has with Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
- Text copied to your clipboard stays on your computer until you paste it or copy something else.

## What Bernie stores, and for how long

- **Page text:** held inside Chrome for a few seconds while the Claude tab loads, then deleted. If Claude doesn't load, it is deleted after 2 minutes. It is also cleared when you close Chrome.
- **Your settings:** your chosen request (such as "Summarise it") and where to open Claude. These are saved in Chrome's own sync storage, linked to your Chrome profile. No page content is saved here.

Bernie stores nothing else and keeps no history of pages you have sent.

## Permissions Bernie uses

- **activeTab and scripting:** to read the current page, only when you ask.
- **contextMenus:** to add Bernie to the right-click menu.
- **storage:** to remember your settings and briefly hold page text, as explained above.
- **Access to claude.ai:** to paste the page into the new Claude chat that Bernie opens. Bernie does not read your Claude chats.

## Your choices

- You can change your settings at any time in the Bernie popup.
- To remove all Bernie data, uninstall the extension. This deletes its stored settings.
- Please think before sending pages with sensitive or confidential information to Claude, and follow your organisation's guidance on using AI tools.

## Changes to this policy

If Bernie changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the effective date changed. The full history of changes is visible on this GitHub page.
