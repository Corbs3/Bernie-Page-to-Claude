# Bernie privacy policy

Bernie does not collect, store or send your data anywhere.

- It only reads a page when you click the button, use the right-click menu or the shortcut.
- The page text is held briefly inside Chrome, then pasted into the Claude tab it opens for you.
- Nothing is sent until you choose to press Enter in Claude.
- Your chosen prompt setting is saved in Chrome sync storage. Nothing else is saved.
- Bernie has no analytics, tracking or outside servers.
